- Travel time from vehicle's current position to request's pickup point (TVPU) done!
- Expected cost for servicing the request (COST)
- Request demand (is 0 for charging actions) (DEM)
- Duration: travel time from the pickup to the dropoff location of the request (DUR)
- Expected slack of the request (window end time - current time - earliest arrival time out of all vehicles); measure of urgency (SLACK)
- Crowdedness of request: average travel time from its pickup point to all the pickup and dropoff points of the other requests (CRD)
- Vehicle's remaining capacity (RQ)
- Vehicle's remaining charge (RT)
- Vehicle slack: the minimal slack of the requests in the vehicle's planned route (VSLACK)
- Current distance of the vehicle from the closest charging station (TVC)
