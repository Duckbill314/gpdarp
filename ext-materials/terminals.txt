- Distance from vehicle's current position to request's pickup point
- Distance from vehicle's current position to request's dropoff point (a vehicle may not want to serve a request whose dropoff is close to its current location)
- Current distance of the vehicle from the closest charging station
- Future distance of the vehicle from the closest charging station
- Vehicle capacity
- Request demand (is 0 for charging actions)
- Vehicle's current charge
- Expected cost for servicing the request
- Expected penalty for servicing the request
- Expected slack for serving the request (window end time - current time - time taken to get to request); measure of urgency
- Time to wait for the best different vehicle to service the request
